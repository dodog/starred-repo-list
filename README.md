# Starred repositories list
Simple userscript to list your starred repositories

## Instalation
Install it in Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey by opening the file, or by pasting it into a new script.

## What it does
On any github.com page, a **★ List my stars** button appears in the bottom-right corner. Clicking it fetches all your starred repos and shows them in a panel, alphabetically sorted and linked to its page.

## How to use
- **Live filtering**: the list narrows as you type
- **Cache**: List is saved in your browser’s local storage after the first fetch and reused for 24 hours.
- **Refresh butto**n: Press it right after starring something to fetch a fresh list.
- **Auto-expiry**: after 24 hours the next click fetches again, so new stars show up on their own within a day.
- **Click outside or ESC to close**
